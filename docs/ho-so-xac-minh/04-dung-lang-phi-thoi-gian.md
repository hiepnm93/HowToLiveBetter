## Mục 4, 8: Buehler, Griffin & Ross (1994)

- Xuất xứ số liệu (thân bài, Study 1): “respondents predicted, on average, that they would finish in 33.9 days, but they actually took 55.5 days … Fewer than one third of the respondents (29.7%) finished in the time they reported as their most accurate prediction.”
- Xuất xứ số liệu (thân bài, Study 4): “…in the recall-relevant condition (60.0%) than in the recall and control conditions (38.1% and 29.3%, respectively)”; “Note, n = 41, 42, and 40 in the control, recall, and recall-relevant conditions”; tóm tắt: “In Study 4, the bias was eliminated for participants instructed to connect past experiences with their predictions.”
- Xuất xứ số liệu (thân bài, Study 2, mục 8): “A subset of the subjects (n = 62) reported having external deadlines … a majority of these subjects (80.6%) finished the projects in time to meet their deadlines … only 38.7% of these subjects finished in the predicted time … their reported completion times were strongly associated with the deadlines (r = .82, p < .001)”; giữa predictions và deadline chỉ có r = .23.
